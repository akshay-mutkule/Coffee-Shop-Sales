# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard

A professional **Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and store performance. This interactive dashboard provides valuable business insights through dynamic visualizations and KPIs.

## 🚀 Project Overview

The Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard helps businesses monitor their sales performance by visualizing important metrics such as:

- Total Sales
- Total Footfall
- Average Order Value
- Average Bill
- Store-wise Performance
- Product-wise Sales
- Category Distribution
- Order Size Distribution
- Hourly Sales Trend
- Weekend Order Analysis

The dashboard enables managers and business owners to make data-driven decisions and identify growth opportunities.

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 💰 Total Sales | Overall revenue generated |
| 👥 Total Footfall | Total customer visits |
| 🛒 Average Order | Average items per order |
| 💵 Average Bill | Average bill amount per customer |

## 📊 Business Insights

- Peak sales occur during morning business hours.
- Coffee contributes the highest share of overall sales.
- Hell's Kitchen generates the highest revenue among all stores.
- Large and Regular orders account for the majority of customer purchases.
- Barista Espresso is the top-selling product.
- Customer traffic varies significantly across weekdays.
